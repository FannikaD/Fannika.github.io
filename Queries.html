FAQ

Does delivery cost extra?

We do not charge extra for delivery within Auckland of 50 pallets or more.

Do you have a minimum order?

We do not have a minimum order, however we do charge extra for delivery of less than 50 pallets.

Do you do recurring deliveries?

Yes, we can arrange recurring deliveries for our regular customers.

What are your business hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 7:00am to 7:00pm and Saturday from 9:00am to 12:00pm.

Do you offer samples to look at?

Yes, we can provide samples for you to inspect before making a purchase.

Do you offer custom pallets?

Yes, we can provide custom pallets to meet your specific requirements. Please contact us to discuss your needs.

Do you offer financing options?

Yes, we can discuss financing options for larger orders. Please contact us for more information.
