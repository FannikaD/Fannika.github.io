Choosing the right pallet

What is the difference between a two-way and four-way pallet?

A two-way pallet is designed for fork entry from two opposite sides. A four-way pallet allows entry from all four sides, although the type of access can vary by design. Four-way access can make warehouse handling and truck loading more flexible. We can help confirm which style suits your forklifts, pallet jacks and storage layout.

What is the difference between a lightweight, standard and heavy-duty pallet?

The difference is generally in the pallet's construction, strength, weight capacity and intended number of uses. A lightweight pallet may suit one-way transport or lighter loads, while a heavy-duty pallet may be required for dense products, repeated use or warehouse racking. The correct choice depends on both the load weight and how the pallet will be handled.

Can your pallets be used in warehouse racking?

Some can, but not every pallet is suitable for racking. Racking places different forces on a pallet than floor storage, and the correct design and load rating are essential. Tell us your rack configuration, pallet size, product weight and how the load is distributed so we can identify a suitable option.

Do you supply pallets for automated or robotic systems?

Yes, subject to the system's exact requirements. Automated handling equipment can be very specific about dimensions, tolerances, deck design, entry points and pallet condition. Send us the technical specification from the equipment provider and we can recommend or source a matching pallet.

How much weight can a pallet hold?

Capacity depends on the pallet's design, material, condition and how it is being used. Static load, dynamic load and racking load ratings are different and should not be treated as interchangeable. Tell us the product weight, load distribution and storage method so we can help you select a pallet with an appropriate rating.

Are wooden or plastic pallets better?

Neither material is automatically better for every job. Wooden pallets are widely used and often more economical. Plastic pallets offer consistent dimensions, moisture resistance and easy cleaning, and are often substantially lighter than comparable wooden pallets. That lower weight can produce valuable freight savings when shipping charges are calculated by the kilogram, which makes plastic pallets particularly attractive for airfreight. The right option depends on your operation, and we are happy to compare suitable choices.

Do you supply food-grade or hygienic pallets?

Yes. We supply food-grade pallet options, including plastic pallets that are easy to clean and highly resistant to moisture. We also regularly have high-quality pallets that are virtually new or have only been used once. Because hygiene and regulatory requirements differ between industries, tell us the standard your business requires and we will match you with an appropriate option.

Pallets for exporting

Do you sell export pallets?

Yes. We supply wooden and plastic pallet options that may be suitable for export. The destination country, shipment type and importing rules can affect what is required, so tell us where the goods are going and confirm the requirements with your freight forwarder or customs adviser.

What does ISPM 15 or heat-treated mean?

ISPM 15 is an international phytosanitary standard applying to many wooden packaging materials used in international trade. Compliant wooden pallets are treated and marked to help reduce the movement of pests between countries. Requirements and exemptions can vary, so confirm your destination and shipping requirements before ordering.

Are plastic pallets suitable for export?

Plastic pallets are often used for export because the international treatment rules applying to solid wood packaging generally do not apply to plastic. They can be a practical option for one-way shipments, but size, load rating, destination requirements and customer specifications still need to be checked.

Can I use any heat-stamped pallet for export?

Not necessarily. A visible stamp alone should not be the only check. The pallet needs to be appropriate for the load, in acceptable condition and compliant with the requirements of the destination and shipping provider. Let us know where the shipment is going and what your freight company has specified

Sustainability and service

Why buy second-hand pallets?

Buying suitable second-hand pallets can reduce cost and extend the working life of existing materials. It keeps reusable pallets in circulation and can reduce demand for newly manufactured products. The key is choosing a used pallet that is still appropriate for the load and application.

What happens to the pallets you collect?

Collected pallets are sorted by size, type and condition. Suitable pallets are returned to service, while others may go through our in-house repair and alteration department or be separated into appropriate recycling streams. This keeps usable pallets and materials in circulation for as long as practical.
